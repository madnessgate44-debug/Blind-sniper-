# Workflow: Idea → Contract → Acceptance

**Adapted from:** 0xenzyme intake + SUNRNEHUI intent-to-contract synthesis.

1. Capture the user's raw intent without silently removing ambiguity.
2. Inspect repository instructions and current state.
3. Identify outcome, observable done-when, anti-success conditions, scope, non-goals, constraints, ownership and evidence.
4. Label facts, assumptions, recommended defaults and open decisions.
5. Stop for material open decisions; do not dispatch on unresolved scope, cost, security or authority.
6. Create the task contract and acceptance criteria.
7. Route to execution only after the contract is reviewable and sufficiently complete.

Preview/intake is read-only unless the user explicitly asks to record or promote an idea.
